WHAT'S NEW, OCT 5, 2026
JJG Vocals 1.1.0 adds HQ mode (4x oversampling), a THD on/off switch for the console harmonics, a Clean Slate preset, a reordered top bar and smoother knob moves. All three plugins get a Lucas Arens preset pack. Preset list fixes: a click now loads the preset in every DAW and rows highlight on hover. Save and Rename dialogs open on top of the plugin. Windows: fixes for DAW freezes when a plugin was removed, activation fixes, and the plugins now show in proper VST3 folders. Windows is updated in this release too.
  1. Run the installer.
  2. Tick the plugins you want: JJG Vocals, Salt, Schmear.
  3. Tick the formats your DAW uses: VST3, AU (macOS) or AAX.
  4. Open the plugin in your DAW and click Start Free Trial. It takes you to Moonbase to activate your 28-day trial, no card needed. Schmear is free, but you still need to "buy" it for $0 through checkout so the license is on your account. Without that, it won't activate.
